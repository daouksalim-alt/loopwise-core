Make one puzzle your own.

Change this working example directly in your browser. The library generates and checks the question; you control the page.

  1. Under HTML, change the heading or the seed input's my-first-puzzle value. Under JavaScript, try changing how the answer feedback is displayed.
  2. Choose Run preview and answer a question.
  3. Download your page or share what worked or blocked you.

Your edits stay in this tab; they are not saved or sent anywhere. Download before closing. The preview blocks network requests and runs separately from the editor. This is a maintainer example, not evidence of outside adoption.
